# ギミック全16項目のデザイン見本の生成プロンプト

組み込みのimagegenで作成した方針検討用の画像。A・Bは同じデザインを、描き込みの量を変えて比較したもの。現在の仮デザインは構図や形の参照に使わず、オブジェクトの役割から形を考え直した。

Aの参照画像は、`docs/sprites/legacy-character-poses.png`（描き方）、`assets/characters/jump-creature-rest.png` と `assets/characters/red-umbrella-rest.png`（デザインを引き継ぐ生物）。Aの修正は初回の生成画像、Bは修正後のAを編集対象とした。

最終画像は `gimmicks-a.png` と `gimmicks-b.png`。役割・状態・バリエーションの一覧は `../gimmick-design.md`。これらはデザイン見本であり、当たり判定や動作を設定したゲーム用素材ではない。

## Aの初回生成

```text

Asset type: a complete illustrated game-gimmick design inventory, option A, for the 2D side-view game Ninja Buttobi-kun Galaxy.
Primary request: draw ALL SIXTEEN numbered panels described below on one spacious 2048 x 2048 square contact sheet. EXACTLY four columns and four rows, IDs 01 through 16 in reading order. This is a fresh FUNCTION-FIRST REDESIGN, not a recoloring of prototype assets. Each silhouette and physical cue should make its action understandable even without reading its caption. Friendly, slightly odd space technology drawn in the same modest hand-drawn black-outline style as the original ninja.
Input images: Image 1 is ONLY the original drawing-style reference, the original ninja pose sheet. Do not copy that sheet's layout, wording, or character into the numbered inventory. Images 2 and 3 are the current green jump creature and red one-eyed umbrella; these two designs are EXEMPT from redesign and must be reproduced faithfully in panels 09 and 10. They have the same appearance in A and B. Do not redesign their faces, proportions, colors or silhouette.
Composition: plain opaque very pale warm-gray background, generous gaps between all sixteen panels, no scenery behind the objects, no decorative frames. Clear top title EXACTLY "A｜前作寄り". Each panel has the EXACT numbered short Japanese caption below its art. No long paragraphs. Give each panel enough room for a clear front/side orthographic game object and small state/direction variants where specified. These are game art sketches, not app icons, photoreal products or an isometric sprite atlas.
Style A: slightly uneven but confident dark pencil/ink outlines; broad mostly flat color fills; very few simple seams; only tiny secondary tones to distinguish necessary parts. Sparse restrained indigo, muted coral, sage, cream, gray, violet. Avoid gradients, paper texture, gloss, white highlight streaks, chrome, pixel art, 3D lighting, neon, smooth corporate vector icons, intricate sci-fi panel grids, realistic textures or excessively cute redesigns. Preserve a gently handmade look. Objects should remain legible when reduced.

THE SIXTEEN PANELS, with captions that must be spelled exactly:
01 "01 重力ゲート": two OPEN pass-through gravity portals, one warm coral with a clear UP arrow and one cool cyan with a clear DOWN arrow. Redesigned as two short disconnected arc/corner emitters around visibly empty space, NOT opaque rectangular panels, NOT solid doors. Beneath them add a tiny example of the same portal following a dotted horizontal path and a tiny dotted vertical-path diagram; movement path arrows are OUTSIDE the portal and visually distinct from its internal gravity arrow. Fixed and moving portals share one visual family. Do not imply teleportation, damage, blocking walls or a physical rail.
02 "02 ダッシュ回復": a floating mint-colored speed-charge pickup shaped like a small compact comet/capsule, with TWO bold forward chevrons and two short speed streaks. No pedestal. It is clearly a collectible burst of movement energy, NOT the old plain green diamond, NOT a medical heart or a weapon.
03 "03 空中ジャンプ": a floating violet jump-charge pickup shaped like a compact BOOT with a visibly springy sole and a small curved hop cue. Strongly different silhouette from 02. 04 "04 発射ばね": a real visible coil spring between a base and a FLAT contact paddle, a bold direction chevron on the paddle. The main example launches upward; small rotated copies launch sideways and downward. One usable face only, spring compression is visible. Not the prototype's freestanding green arrow. This launches on contact, not by pressing an action button.
05 "05 全方向バンパー": a plump round rubber bounce cushion with a few outward direction marks and a small incoming-to-outgoing bent trajectory cue outside it. Fully rounded reactive surface, NO one-way paddle, NO fixed giant arrow, NO old concentric donut target. Side-view and floating; collisions can bounce from every side.
06 "06 風ゾーン": a short simple vent/fan mouth with unmistakable flowing curved AIR STREAMS. Show a horizontal leftward example and a rotated upward example. Sparse arrowheads and light dashed outer limits show the affected air volume; no opaque box across the field. The fan is a visual emitter, not a new interactable device, and the drawn stream must not look like fire, electricity or a damaging beam.
07 "07 軽い箱": a pale cream/sage HOLLOW transport case with a broad flat top, a few large open slots and obvious recessed hand grips on its sides. Light and airy silhouette, carryable but still a usable physical box. Not the existing taped cardboard carton; no parachute, wings or extra power.
08 "08 重い箱": a squat dense dark indigo/gray reinforced transport block with a broad flat top and obvious side lifting grips. Solid filled mass, thick bottom band and two large simple fasteners make it look heavy, in contrast to 07. Not the existing copper kettlebell and not a weapon. Both 07 and 08 can be put on a weight switch.
09 "09 緑の生物": faithfully show the existing green big-headed creature with pointed ears, oval eyes, cheek marks and small feet from Image 2, unchanged. A small separate carrying/hop diagram underneath can suggest its held extra-jump ability WITHOUT adding an arrow or equipment onto its body.
10 "10 傘": faithfully show the existing red triangular one-eyed tongue-out umbrella creature from Image 3, unchanged, with its central handle/foot. Small separate downward slow-fall lines and an upward airflow cue may be placed beside it, not painted onto the character. No space-suit redesign.
11 "11 重量スイッチ": a low broad SQUARE weighing tray with a simple BOX pictogram on its front and a small off/on indicator. A little example shows a light case resting on the lowered tray and a tiny adjacent raised empty tray for OFF. Distinct from the round player goal in 12. NO footprints on this tray: the player's body alone does NOT activate it. Do not portray it as heavy-only; a light case can also activate it. No new door connection.
12 "12 到着台": a low ROUND cream/gold finish platform with TWO FOOTPRINTS plainly marked on its standing plate, plus a small checkered finish pennant integrated at its edge. 13 "13 ロックケース": a mostly clear rounded protective hood enclosing the SAME round footprint finish platform of 12, with a lock symbol and a little three-task checklist strip. Preserve the platform inside, no treasure chest, no opaque glass fill, no old box-shaped shiny glass showcase.
14 "14 出口の壁": an unmistakably SOLID closed side-view space shutter/hatch with a small matching footprint/finish pictogram, paired with the same aperture clear after the obstacle disappears. Closed blocks passage; open allows passage. Clearly different from the airy pass-through portals of 01. No laser, spikes or damage effect, no separate collectible key.
15 "15 落とし穴": a short FLOOR GAP and an inverted CEILING GAP, both with simple orange warning chevrons only on the existing ledges, empty dark space in the middle and small gravity-direction falling cues. Not a new enemy, trap machine, spike pit or death beam. The opening itself is the hazard.
16 "16 帰り道の橋": two small side-view diagrams of the same gap, before with NO bridge and after with a solid flat bridge filling the opening. Broad simple joined panels and end tabs; same readable edge style as the physical boxes/platforms. This bridge appears after the room is cleared, it is NOT a moving platform, drawbridge, conveyor or player-operated lever. No hinges, motors, sliding arrows or invented interactive control.

Constraints: include every ID and no additional numbered item. All art must be separated within its own panel. Favor large clear functional forms over tiny symbols. Labels aid identification but the artwork itself should communicate the action. Only existing mechanics; redesign appearance, not gameplay. This is rendering-density option A; we will later edit the same sheet to B without changing object designs.

The lower ceiling-gap example must have an UP pointing falling arrow, and the falling-motion strokes belong BELOW that arrow so it unmistakably shows falling UP into the ceiling opening. 2. In panel 13 "13 ロックケース", keep the large LOCKED protective hood, checklist and footprint platform intact. In particular 01 should have matte coral and cyan emitters, 07 and 08 should have simple flat case faces, 12 a matte gold footprint plate, 14 simple matte hatch faces. 4. In panel 12, make the right hand PRESSED state clearly lower than the left READY state by seating its footprint plate flush into the same round base. Keep both designs otherwise identical, with no coil spring or new mechanism.
